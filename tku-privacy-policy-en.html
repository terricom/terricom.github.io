Thau-Khak-Uah (頭殼活) Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This policy explains how "Thau-Khak-Uah" (the "App") handles your information. The App is developed and operated by TerriDev ("we", "us").

Summary: the App has no accounts and no cloud database. Gameplay happens directly between devices on the same local network, and no game data passes through our servers. The only third-party service we use is Google AdMob rewarded ads.

1. How the App works: no cloud server

The App uses a "host-device local server" architecture: the player who creates a room (the host) runs a local network server directly on their own phone, and other players' devices join by connecting over the same Wi-Fi network. All room state — the player list, number cards, reveal results, ranks, and so on — exists only in the host device's memory. When the host disconnects or closes the App, the room disappears. This data is never sent to any server or database operated by us or any third party.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and personal information: the App does not require an account and does not collect your name, phone number, email address, or other personally identifying information.
  • Camera permission: used only to scan a QR code to join a room. Scanning is processed in real time on-device; no image or video is stored or transmitted.
  • Local device storage: the App stores your daily "free round" usage status locally on your device, so it can tell whether you've already used your free round within the last 24 hours. This data stays on your device and is never synced or sent to us.
  • Local-network gameplay data: during a game, nicknames, answers, reveal order, and ranks are transmitted only between devices on the same local network to keep the game running. We have no access to and never store this data.

3. Advertising and third-party services (Google AdMob)

The App offers an "ad continuation" feature: once your daily free round is used up, the host can choose to watch a rewarded video ad, served by Google AdMob, to unlock the next round. When AdMob serves an ad, it may collect a device advertising identifier (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location, and device information, for ad delivery, personalization, and measurement. This processing is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The App also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent management form where required (for example, to users in the EU or UK), letting you choose whether to allow personalized ads. You can adjust your consent choice through that form at any time.

4. Data retention

Game data exists only in the host device's memory and is automatically discarded when the room closes or the App is exited. We do not separately back up or retain any game content.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from any user. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App without a parent or guardian's consent, please contact us at the email below and we will address it promptly.

6. Your choices

  • You can disable the camera permission at any time in your device settings. Doing so disables QR-code scanning to join a room, but you can still join by entering a room code manually.
  • For ad personalization, you can adjust your device's ad settings (such as "Limit Ad Tracking") or use the Google consent management form described above.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to check back periodically.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, please contact us at: ting2discover@gmail.com
